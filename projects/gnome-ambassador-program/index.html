GNOME Ambassador Program

Building the GNOME Ambassador Program to empower local community leaders worldwide to represent and grow the GNOME ecosystem

Community Outreach Global Open Source Non-profit
June 2026 – June 2028

Overview

Building the GNOME Ambassador Program — a community initiative to empower local ambassadors worldwide to represent GNOME in their regions, organize local events, and grow the ecosystem through personal connections.

Program Goals

  • Local representation — enabling GNOME advocates to represent the project in their own communities and languages
  • Community growth — building regional communities that welcome new contributors and users
  • Outreach — supporting ambassadors in organizing local events, meetups, and educational activities
  • Onboarding — helping newcomers find their way into the GNOME community through local, trusted connections
  • Global reach — extending GNOME’s presence beyond the usual conference circuit into regional communities

My Role

Leading the design and rollout of the program: defining the ambassador role, building the application and onboarding process, establishing expectations and support structures, and coordinating with the GNOME Engagement Team.

Impact

  • Creating a sustainable, community-driven model for GNOME outreach worldwide
  • Building the foundation for a global network of local GNOME communities
